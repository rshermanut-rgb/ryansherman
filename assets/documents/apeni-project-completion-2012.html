Apeni school water project

Completion note based on Ryan Sherman’s email to Appropriate Projects, 23 November 2012.

The project installed two new hand-washing sinks and a new well at Apeni Public School in Georgia’s Lagodekhi region.

Local workmen dug the well and rock drains and installed the pump, piping, and holding tank on the school roof. A PE teacher helped direct the labour and decide where the sinks should go.

The sinks were installed outside, on either side of the walkway connecting the school’s two sections. One served the route to the teachers’ toilets; the other served the student toilets. Ryan reported that both were used frequently for washing hands.

Ryan also reported making a short video about the project and interviewing another Peace Corps volunteer about Appropriate Projects work. The original email offered to upload the video; no published video link has been verified.

This page is a concise transcription-based summary of the completion email. The project’s original budget and photographs are linked from its portfolio entry.
